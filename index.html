How teams share it

No logins and no software to install. Work saves in your browser as you go. When estimating hands off to the PM, or the PM hands off to accounting, use Save file at the top of any page and send the file. The next person opens it with Open file.

Every workbook also exports to Excel with live formulas. The pay application downloads as a formatted summary sheet and continuation sheet, ready for the lender. Prefer to work in Excel? Download the estimating template or the pay application template.

For your company, the same tools get your cost codes, your rates, your forms, and, if you want it, a shared database so everyone works in one live file.
